PROPRIETARY ANALYTICS FRAMEWORK

AA+ Programs

Team edcarSky’s proprietary AA+ analytics and channel-activation framework helps creators revive dormant analytics, strengthen genuine audience activity and make performance signals clearer. It serves YouTube, Facebook, TikTok and other social platforms, not one network alone.

Explore AA+ services

AA+ brings together channel and content diagnostics, audience fit, topic research, title and thumbnail packaging, opening-hook review, retention, engagement, traffic sources, returning viewers, SEO and monetization readiness.

It is designed to help creators understand the signals that matter to platform discovery and audience response. Platform systems make their own decisions. No ranking, reach or monetization result is guaranteed.

ORGANIC GROWTH & PROMOTION

SSP+ Programs

Structured social-sharing and video-promotion support that helps creators put their content in front of relevant audiences while keeping organic and paid activity transparent.

Our organic-growth approach focuses on genuine discovery and real audience activity. We do not sell bots, fake subscribers, artificial watch hours or engagement manipulation.
