PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This notice explains how Edcar Aguirre Tenedero processes information submitted through the professional inquiry form on this website.

Information collected

The form requests your name, company or organization, professional affiliation or position, email address, contact number, inquiry type and message. Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your inquiry.

Purpose and lawful basis

Information is used to review and respond to employment, client, collaboration and portfolio inquiries, and to maintain a record of professional communications. Processing is based on your consent given through the form and, where applicable, steps requested by you before entering a professional relationship or legitimate interests that do not override your rights.

Access, storage and disclosure

Inquiry submissions are private and are intended to be accessible only to the website owner through an authenticated administration account. The website uses a third-party hosting and database provider configured by the owner. Information is not displayed publicly. It is not sold. It is not shared with other parties except service providers needed to operate the website, when legally required, or with your permission.

Website analytics

If you accept the analytics notice, the website records the page path, visit time, a randomly generated browser identifier, referring page and browser user-agent string. The portfolio does not request or store your raw IP address through this visitor log. The identifier is stored in your browser local storage to distinguish repeat visits on that browser. Selecting Reject prevents this optional visitor event from being sent. You may clear the choice and identifier by clearing this website’s local storage.

Retention

Inquiry records should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage the professional relationship and meet applicable legal requirements. The owner should periodically review and securely delete records that no longer serve a purpose.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, object to or withdraw consent where applicable, and raise concerns about its processing. To make a request, contact edcarsky@yahoo.com.

Testimonials

Feedback submitted through the inquiry form is private by default. A comment will not appear as a public testimonial unless the owner obtains appropriate permission and approves it for publication. Contact details and private inquiry records are not published with testimonials.

Security and contact

Reasonable organizational and technical measures should be maintained, including strong unique passwords, multi-factor authentication where available, restricted administrator access and secure configuration of the database and file storage. For privacy questions, contact Edcar Aguirre Tenedero at edcarsky@yahoo.com.
